Good to know

Questions, answered

Can I bring my kids?
Absolutely. Just add them to your RSVP so we can plan activities, seating, and kid-friendly food.
Can I bring a plus-one?
Yes. Add everyone coming with you under "Adults" in the RSVP so we have the right headcount.
Is it a surprise?
No surprises here. Rachana knows and is very much looking forward to seeing everyone.
I can't make it. Can I still send something?
We'll miss you. The registry ships straight to our home, and a note on the wishes wall would mean a lot; we're printing those for the nursery.
Should I wrap my gift?
No need. A gift bag or the shipping box is perfect, and it makes the opening quicker so we spend more time with you.
What if I'm running late or my plans change?
Come whenever you can; it's an open afternoon from 11 to 3. If your plans change, update your RSVP from the same link, or text Sahit.
